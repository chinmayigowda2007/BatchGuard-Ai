# BatchGuard-
AI-powered food business assistant for batch tracking, compliance guidance, production costing, and market readiness for micro food processors.

### 3. Research Relevance

These references support the key problem areas addressed by BATCHGUARD AI:

- **Regulatory awareness:** Helping micro food processors understand relevant food-business registration, labelling and hygiene requirements.
- **Digital record management:** Organising ingredient information, production details and batch records.
- **Cost transparency:** Helping producers calculate production costs and cost per unit.
- **Market readiness:** Preparing structured product information and buyer-facing documentation.
- **Technology accessibility:** Exploring open-source tools to develop an affordable and accessible solution.

### 4. Important Note

The government links are provided as references for understanding applicable regulations and support initiatives. The GitHub repositories are technical references and are not claimed as original BATCHGUARD AI development.

BATCHGUARD AI is designed to assist producers with documentation and preparation. It does not replace official FSSAI registration, regulatory approval, laboratory testing or professional food-safety assessment. All compliance-related outputs require appropriate human verification.
